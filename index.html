About

I am a semiconductor engineer with industry experience in FPGA products, heterogeneous integration, product engineering, and system-level analysis. My research interests center on System-Technology Co-Optimization (STCO) and heterogeneous integration, particularly architecture and workload benchmarking and EDA methodologies enabling cross-layer co-design.

Research

Current interests include workload-aware evaluation of monolithic, 2.5D, and 3D integration strategies and cross-layer design methodologies for heterogeneous systems.
